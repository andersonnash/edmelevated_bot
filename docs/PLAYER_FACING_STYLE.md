# Player-Facing Style

Player-facing copy should be clear first and atmospheric second. Use EDM and nightlife language where it adds character, but avoid cryptic sci-fi wording that obscures what happened or what the player should do next.

## Canonical Terms

- **Cash** for the player's spendable balance.
- **Scene Reputation** for city-wide reputation.
- **DJ Reputation** for DJ career reputation.
- **Venue income** for income generated by owned venues.
- **Equipment rental income** for income generated by owned equipment.
- **Passive income** only as an umbrella term when multiple income sources are shown together.
- **Ready to collect** when a reward is available.
- **Settle a show** and **`/collect_show`** for collecting a completed show's results.
- **Show staff** for staff hired for one show; **venue staff** for staff attached to a venue.
- Keep **tickets sold**, **projected walk-ins**, and **total attendance** distinct.

## Response Structure

When practical, present information in this order:

1. What happened.
2. The cost, reward, or effect.
3. The player's updated state.
4. The next available action or cooldown.

## Costs and Payouts

- Show the exact cost before confirming a purchase and again in the result.
- When possible, insufficient-funds messages should show both the required amount and current cash.
- Describe payout timing accurately. DJs and show staff are paid only when the owner settles the completed show with `/collect_show`.

## Visual Language

- Cyan: navigation and neutral information.
- Magenta: music and shows.
- Amber: choices and risk.
- Green: completion and payments.
- Red: failures, losses, and closures.
- Violet: progression.
- Use emojis sparingly. Avoid decorating every title, field, and button.
- Use consistent title case and avoid unnecessary all-caps copy.
- Buttons should use short, plain action labels.

## Guidance and Errors

- Footers should add useful next-step information instead of repeating the embed.
- Error messages should explain how the player can resolve the problem.
- Prefer direct command references when a command is the next step.
