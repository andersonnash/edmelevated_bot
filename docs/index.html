New Players

Start here

The fastest way to understand the game is to follow this early path. Use /profile any time you feel lost, and /help to browse the full command guide inside Discord.

1

Run /profile and /help

Use /profile to see your cash, XP, reputation, income, and next objective. Use /help to browse commands by category.

2

Earn starter cash

Use /work, /crate_dig, or /rave_story.

3

Buy your first gear

Use /buy_equipment to start earning equipment rental income.

4

Take a DJ booking

Use /bookings to unlock your DJ profile and raise your booking fee.

5

Buy a venue

Save up for your first event space with /buy_venue.

6

Throw your first show

Create, promote, staff, and settle payouts from your event.

FAQ

Common questions

Where do I see all commands?

Use /help in Discord. The help menu groups commands by shows, games, venues, DJs, staff, economy, and extras.

Why can’t I take a booking?

You need to own equipment first. Buy starter gear with /buy_equipment.

Why can’t I create a show?

You need to own a venue before you can create shows.

How do I earn passive income?

Buy equipment and venues, then use /collect when income is ready.

How do DJs get paid?

DJs build value through bookings and lineups. Show payouts are settled when the owner collects a completed show.

Is the game finished?

No. This is an early playtest. Balance, rewards, cooldowns, and commands may change.
