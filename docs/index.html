🎧 DJ Career

After buying gear, players can use /bookings to take DJ opportunities. Bookings reward cash, XP, scene reputation, and DJ reputation.

Your first booking creates your DJ profile. As your DJ reputation and bookings grow, your booking fee increases.

🏙 Venues & Shows

Venues generate passive income and let you create shows. Shows can be promoted, staffed, and built around other DJs.

Completed shows pay out when the owner uses /collect_show.

Command Guide

Main commands

Starter Commands

  • /profile — View your progress and next objective.
  • /help — Open the in-Discord command guide.
  • /work — Earn reliable starter cash.
  • /crate_dig — Dig for rewards and possible show demand.
  • /rave_story — Play short choice-based scene events.

Equipment

  • /buy_equipment — Buy gear that earns passive income.
  • /my_equipment — View your owned gear and income.
  • /collect — Collect passive income from gear and venues.

DJ Career

  • /bookings — Take DJ career opportunities.
  • /dj_profile — View DJ reputation, bookings, and booking fee.
  • /top_djs — View the top DJs in the city.

Shows

  • /create_show — Create a show at one of your venues.
  • /my_shows — View and manage your shows.
  • /promote_show — Spend cash to increase walk-ins.
  • /collect_show — Collect profits from completed shows.

Lineups & Staff

  • /add_lineup — Add another user as a DJ.
  • /show_lineup — View DJs and staff on a show.
  • /hire_staff — Hire temporary show staff.
  • /my_jobs — View shows where you were hired.

Venues

  • /buy_venue — Buy your first event space.
  • /my_venues — View venues, upgrades, staff, and income.
  • /upgrade_venue — Upgrade Bar, Security, or Production.
  • /hire_venue_staff — Hire permanent venue staff.
  • /venue_insurance — Reduce venue incident risk.

FAQ

Common questions

Where do I see all commands?

Use /help in Discord. The help menu groups commands by shows, games, venues, DJs, staff, economy, and extras.

Why can’t I take a booking?

You need to own equipment first. Buy starter gear with /buy_equipment.

Why can’t I create a show?

You need to own a venue before you can create shows.

How do I earn passive income?

Buy equipment and venues, then use /collect when income is ready.

How do DJs get paid?

DJs build value through bookings and lineups. Show payouts are settled when the owner collects a completed show.

Is the game finished?

No. This is an early playtest. Balance, rewards, cooldowns, and commands may change.
